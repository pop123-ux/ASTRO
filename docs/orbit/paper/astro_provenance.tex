\section{ASTRO Baseline Provenance}
ASTRO entered the ORBIT campaign as a pre-existing frozen baseline selected during the
preceding ASTRO development study, before any ORBIT result was observed. In the historical
124M/900-step targeted mechanism study, the selected ASTRO recipe achieved a mean loss
margin versus Muon of approximately $-0.2208$ across three shared configurations, compared
with $-0.2137$ for ASTRO-Muon-Betas and $-0.2047$ for the gamma-zero control. At one shared
configuration, ASTRO and ASTRO-Muon-Betas were numerically close (5.9208 versus 5.9226).
These measurements document the baseline-selection provenance. The ASTRO recipe was frozen
before the ORBIT campaign and was not retuned using ORBIT results; historical ASTRO
measurements are not pooled with ORBIT's confirmatory statistics.
